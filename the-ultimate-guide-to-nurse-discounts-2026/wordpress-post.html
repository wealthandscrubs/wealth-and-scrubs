Nurses spend a lot on shoes, scrubs, phones, food, and the everyday basics that make long shifts manageable. The good news: several major brands still offer nurse or medical-professional discounts in 2026. The less-good news is that the percentage, eligible products, and verification rules can change without notice.

This guide focuses on offers confirmed on official company websites. Check the linked terms before buying, especially during holiday sales or Nurses Week.

Quick tip: Compare the nurse price with the public sale price before you check out. Many verified discounts cannot be combined with another promotion, and the sale may be better.

How to Verify Your Nurse Status Safely

Programs may use a healthcare email, ID.me, SheerID, a license, an NPI number, or current work documentation. Upload documents only through the retailer's official verification flow. Never send a license, pay statement, or employee badge through social media messages.

  • Make sure your name matches your license and shopping account
  • Use your legal name, not a nickname
  • Check whether the program accepts your exact credential
  • Submit a current, readable document
  • Contact the verification provider instead of creating duplicate accounts

How to Get the Lowest Price

  • Compare the public sale first. A 40% public sale beats a 30% full-price nurse discount.
  • Read exclusions. New releases, sale items, gift cards, taxes, shipping, and prior purchases are often excluded.
  • Check stacking rules. Some verified prices may still work with credit-card rewards, allowed cashback, free loyalty shipping, rebates, or trade-in credit.
  • Set renewal reminders. Wireless offers often require revalidation, while free memberships may convert to paid plans.
  • Keep personal purchases personal. Buying for others, bulk ordering, and resale can violate program terms.

Nurse Discount FAQ

Do nursing students qualify?

Sometimes. A retailer may accept students through a separate student program even when its medical-professional program requires a current license. Check both options.

Do CNAs and medical assistants qualify?

Eligibility varies. Programs that include medical professionals or hospital employees may cover more roles than programs limited to licensed nurses. Use the official eligibility checker.

Can retired or travel nurses qualify?

It depends on the program. Verizon requires active employment; other brands may focus on current license or employment documentation. Travel nurses may need manual review after changing assignments.

Are the discounts available in stores?

Some are. adidas, lululemon, and ASICS offer in-store options, while Nike's medical-professional discount is online and app only.

Start with adidas, Nike, lululemon, and ASICS for work shoes and apparel; compare Verizon and FirstNet for recurring phone savings; check Samsung before a major tech purchase; and apply to Thrive Gives if a free grocery-membership year would help.
