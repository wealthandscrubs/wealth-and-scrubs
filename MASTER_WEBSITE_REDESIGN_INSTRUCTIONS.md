# WEALTH & SCRUBS — MASTER WEBSITE REDESIGN INSTRUCTIONS

Treat the following as permanent project-wide instructions for every change you make to the Wealth & Scrubs website.

Before changing category pages, post templates, article theme styling, or the site-wide color system, also read `CATEGORY_COLOR_SYSTEM.md` and preserve the category-based palette and inheritance rules there.

## 1. Overall Goal

Wealth & Scrubs should look like a legitimate, polished, modern brand — not a generic blog or a website filled with affiliate links.

The website should:

* Catch visitors’ attention quickly.
* Feel clean, professional, trustworthy, and easy to navigate.
* Encourage visitors to continue reading and exploring.
* Help generate income through affiliate marketing, digital products, physical products, and future Wealth & Scrubs products.
* Maintain a recognizable Wealth & Scrubs brand identity throughout the entire website.

Every page should have a purpose. Do not add sections, text, images, buttons, cards, or other elements simply to fill space.

⸻

## 2. Homepage Rules

Keep the homepage SIMPLE.

I do NOT want:

* Large amounts of text.
* Long introductions.
* Too many photos.
* Huge cards.
* Oversized buttons.
* Repetitive category sections.
* The same information repeated elsewhere on the site.
* A cluttered homepage.

The homepage should quickly explain what Wealth & Scrubs offers and give visitors clear reasons to explore further.

Use short, compelling sections and clear calls to action.

The homepage should act as an introduction and navigation point — not contain everything on the website.

⸻

## 3. Engagement on Every Page

Every page and article should be designed to make visitors want to continue reading.

Content should:

* Start with a strong introduction or hook.
* Answer the visitor’s question quickly.
* Be easy to scan.
* Use short paragraphs.
* Use useful headings and subheadings.
* Provide genuinely helpful information.
* Include practical examples when appropriate.
* Include calls to action where they make sense.
* Guide visitors toward related Wealth & Scrubs content.

Avoid unnecessary filler.

Do not write long sections simply for SEO.

Quality and usefulness come first.

⸻

## 4. Absolutely No Plagiarism

All Wealth & Scrubs content must be ORIGINAL.

Never copy:

* Articles.
* Blog posts.
* Product descriptions.
* Affiliate websites.
* Competitor websites.
* Amazon descriptions.
* Manufacturer descriptions.
* Social media content.
* Other online sources.

Research may be used to verify facts, but all final content must be written originally for Wealth & Scrubs.

Do not lightly rewrite or spin another website’s content.

If a source must be referenced, properly attribute it when appropriate.

⸻

## 5. Do Not Repeat Content Across the Website

Before creating or rewriting a page, review existing Wealth & Scrubs content.

Do not create multiple pages that essentially say the same thing.

Avoid repeating:

* Introductions.
* Explanations.
* Product recommendations.
* Lists.
* Affiliate descriptions.
* Calls to action.
* Entire sections from other pages.

Pages may link to each other instead.

If information already exists elsewhere, briefly reference it and direct the visitor to the appropriate page.

Each page should provide its own unique value.

⸻

## 6. Maintain the Wealth & Scrubs Design Everywhere

Every page, article, storefront page, product page, landing page, tool, and future addition must look like it belongs to Wealth & Scrubs.

Maintain the established:

* Pink/white/soft neutral color palette.
* Wealth & Scrubs logo.
* Banner style.
* Typography.
* Heading style.
* Spacing.
* Navigation style.
* Button style.
* Overall clean feminine/professional appearance.

Keep headings and major titles centered when appropriate.

Do not make titles unnecessarily large.

Avoid oversized rounded buttons and oversized cards.

Avoid repetitive same-size card/box layouts throughout the site unless there is a functional reason for them.

Use visual variety while still keeping the site organized: vary widths, use selective emphasis, occasional side-by-side content, text links, smaller feature areas, intentional whitespace, and layouts that can move left, center, or right instead of defaulting to grids of identical cards.

Do not randomly introduce new fonts, colors, layouts, or design styles.

Any new feature I create in the future should automatically follow the Wealth & Scrubs design system.

⸻

## 7. Mobile and Desktop Design

Every change must be reviewed for BOTH:

* Desktop.
* Mobile.

The site should not simply be a desktop design squeezed onto a phone.

Check:

* Text size.
* Button size.
* Menu behavior.
* Image sizing.
* Spacing.
* Page width.
* Alignment.
* Loading speed.
* Navigation.
* Affiliate links.
* Product sections.

Mobile visitors should receive the same quality experience as desktop visitors.

⸻

## 8. Affiliate Marketing Rules

Affiliate marketing will be an important part of Wealth & Scrubs.

I will periodically provide updated affiliate links.

When I provide new affiliate links:

1. Store and organize them appropriately.
2. Identify existing pages where each affiliate naturally belongs.
3. Recommend appropriate places to add the links.
4. Add affiliate links only where they are genuinely relevant.
5. Never randomly insert links just because an affiliate program is available.

Do not make Wealth & Scrubs look like a link directory.

Every affiliate recommendation must include useful information explaining things such as:

* What the company/product/service is.
* Why someone might use it.
* Who it may be good for.
* Important features.
* Potential benefits.
* Important limitations when applicable.

Never display only:

Company Name → Affiliate Link

There must be useful context surrounding recommendations.

⸻

## 9. Affiliate Disclosure

Create and maintain a clear affiliate disclosure explaining that Wealth & Scrubs may earn a commission when visitors purchase or sign up through certain links.

The disclosure should clearly explain that:

* Some links are affiliate links.
* Wealth & Scrubs may earn a commission.
* The visitor generally does not pay more because of the commission.

The disclosure should be easy to find.

Appropriate disclosure language should also appear on pages containing affiliate links when required.

Do not hide affiliate relationships.

⸻

## 10. Affiliate Partners Page

Create a dedicated page explaining the brands, companies, services, and affiliate programs Wealth & Scrubs works with.

This should NOT simply be a giant list of company names.

Organize affiliate partners into useful categories such as:

* Money & Saving.
* Shopping & Deals.
* Nursing.
* Travel.
* Lifestyle.
* Side Hustles.
* Financial Tools.
* Other relevant categories.

Each partner should have a short description explaining what it offers and why Wealth & Scrubs visitors may find it useful.

Include affiliate links where appropriate.

Clearly disclose the affiliate relationship.

⸻

## 11. Wealth & Scrubs Storefront

Build a dedicated Wealth & Scrubs storefront that can grow over time.

The storefront should eventually support:

* Wealth & Scrubs digital products.
* Wealth & Scrubs physical products.
* Upcoming products.
* Recommended products.
* Amazon affiliate products.
* Other affiliate products when appropriate.

The storefront should clearly distinguish between:

Wealth & Scrubs Products

and

Products Wealth & Scrubs Recommends

Do not make visitors think an affiliate product is manufactured or sold directly by Wealth & Scrubs if it is not.

⸻

## 12. Amazon Affiliate Products

Amazon affiliate products may be included throughout the website and storefront where relevant.

Do not create pages containing endless random Amazon products.

Amazon recommendations should relate directly to the page topic.

Examples may include:

* Nursing accessories.
* Work essentials.
* Organization products.
* Meal-prep products.
* Travel items.
* Self-care products.
* Money-saving products.
* Home products.

Provide useful information with each recommendation instead of simply displaying a product name and purchase button.

Use my approved Amazon affiliate links or affiliate tracking information.

Never replace my affiliate tracking with a generic Amazon URL.

Follow current Amazon Associates rules and disclosure requirements.

⸻

## 13. Internal Linking

Whenever a page is created or updated, identify relevant Wealth & Scrubs pages that should link together.

Internal links should help visitors naturally move through the site.

Example:

A grocery-saving article might link to:

* Coupon apps.
* Cashback apps.
* Meal planning.
* Grocery rewards.
* Related store deals.

Do not overuse internal links.

Only link where it genuinely helps the reader.

⸻

## 14. SEO

Optimize pages for search engines without damaging readability.

For every important page or article, consider:

* Search intent.
* Page title.
* Meta title.
* Meta description.
* URL structure.
* Heading hierarchy.
* Internal links.
* Image alt text.
* Relevant keywords.
* Structured data/schema when useful.

Do not keyword-stuff.

Do not create repetitive AI-generated SEO content.

The page should primarily be written for people.

⸻

## 15. Trust and Credibility

Do not make exaggerated claims.

Never claim:

* Guaranteed income.
* Guaranteed savings.
* Guaranteed approval.
* Guaranteed investment returns.
* Guaranteed results.

Clearly distinguish between:

* Facts.
* Recommendations.
* Personal opinions.
* Affiliate relationships.
* Examples.

For financial or potentially sensitive topics, use appropriate disclosures where necessary.

⸻

## 16. Performance

Keep the website lightweight and fast.

Avoid:

* Excessive scripts.
* Excessive animations.
* Unnecessarily large images.
* Duplicate libraries.
* Bloated page builders.
* Decorative features that noticeably slow the website.

Optimize images and assets whenever possible.

Do not sacrifice performance simply to make something visually impressive.

⸻

## 17. Accessibility

Maintain reasonable accessibility standards.

Use:

* Sufficient text contrast.
* Readable font sizes.
* Alt text for meaningful images.
* Proper heading structure.
* Descriptive links.
* Keyboard-friendly navigation where possible.

Do not rely exclusively on color to communicate important information.

⸻

## 18. Work One Page at a Time

We will redesign Wealth & Scrubs one page, article, or feature at a time.

For each page:

1. I will explain what I like, dislike, want removed, want added, or want changed.
2. Use my feedback as the primary design direction.
3. Review the existing page.
4. Identify anything that should be preserved.
5. Recommend improvements when something could be better.
6. Implement the approved redesign.
7. Verify mobile and desktop appearance.
8. Verify links.
9. Verify that no duplicate content was introduced.
10. Verify that the page follows these master instructions.

Do not redesign unrelated pages unless required for site-wide consistency.

⸻

## 19. Protect Existing Work

Before making significant changes:

* Create a backup.
* Preserve the current working version.
* Use Git/version control.
* Make clear commits.
* Avoid destructive changes without a recovery path.

Backups must allow us to restore the site if something goes wrong.

Do not permanently delete important existing content or code unless I specifically approve its removal.

⸻

## 20. Git and Version Control

Use Git consistently.

Before major redesign work:

* Confirm the working tree/status.
* Save or commit the current working version.
* Create logical commits for changes.
* Use descriptive commit messages.

Do not combine unrelated changes into one massive commit when avoidable.

Maintain a clear history so previous versions can be restored.

⸻

## 21. NEVER Publish Without My Permission

This rule is mandatory.

You may:

* Write code.
* Edit files.
* Create pages.
* Create drafts.
* Build layouts.
* Add links.
* Test functionality.
* Preview changes.
* Prepare commits.

You may NOT:

* Publish changes to the live website.
* Deploy to production.
* Merge changes that trigger production deployment.
* Push changes that automatically publish the live site.
* Publish articles.
* Publish products.
* Make a page publicly live.

unless I explicitly give final approval.

Before anything becomes live, show me what was changed and wait for my approval.

My approval to edit or redesign something is NOT automatically approval to publish it.

⸻

## 22. Do Not Make Unnecessary Changes

Do not change something simply because you personally prefer another implementation.

If an existing feature:

* Works correctly.
* Matches the Wealth & Scrubs design.
* Performs well.
* Is secure.
* Does not create maintenance problems.

then preserve it unless there is a meaningful reason to change it.

⸻

## 23. Flag Problems Instead of Hiding Them

If you discover:

* Broken links.
* Duplicate pages.
* Missing affiliate disclosures.
* Outdated affiliate links.
* Security problems.
* SEO problems.
* Mobile layout problems.
* Performance problems.
* Duplicate code.
* Conflicting styles.
* Missing images.
* Dead pages.
* Unnecessary pages.

tell me.

Do not quietly ignore problems.

For significant issues, explain:

1. What the problem is.
2. Why it matters.
3. What you recommend doing.

⸻

## 24. Preserve My Decisions

When I make a design, branding, content, navigation, monetization, or structural decision for Wealth & Scrubs, treat that decision as the new project standard unless I later change my mind.

Do not revert previously approved decisions while working on another page.

⸻

## 25. Final Rule

The goal is not simply to make Wealth & Scrubs look better.

The goal is to build a cohesive, useful, trustworthy, engaging, scalable, and income-producing website.

Every change should support at least one of these objectives:

* Improve the visitor experience.
* Increase trust.
* Increase engagement.
* Improve search visibility.
* Increase affiliate conversions.
* Increase product sales.
* Strengthen the Wealth & Scrubs brand.
* Improve performance.
* Make the website easier to maintain.

If a proposed change does not meaningfully improve the website, do not add it.

These instructions apply throughout the entire Wealth & Scrubs redesign unless I specifically override one of them.
