Healthcare workers work hard for their money, so why not earn some of it back? Cashback apps can help nurses save money on groceries, gas, online shopping, and everyday purchases. Here are some of the best cashback apps to consider in 2026.

1. Rakuten

Rakuten offers cashback when shopping through participating stores. Many major retailers participate, making it easy to earn rewards on purchases you already planned to make.

Why Nurses like it:

  • Easy to use
  • Works with hundreds of stores
  • Cashback paid regularly

2. Ibotta

Ibotta focuses heavily on groceries and household items. Simply add offers before shopping and upload your receipt afterward.

Why Nurses like it:

  • Great for families
  • Grocery savings add up quickly
  • Frequent bonus offers

3. Upside

Upside helps users earn cashback on gas purchases and sometimes restaurants and convenience stores.

Why Nurses like it:

  • Useful for long commutes
  • Simple receipt verification
  • Helps reduce fuel costs

4. Fetch

Fetch Rewards turns everyday receipts into points that can be redeemed for gift cards.

Why Nurses like it:

  • Very easy to use
  • Scan almost any receipt
  • Gift card rewards

5. Shopkick

Shopkick rewards users for shopping activities such as scanning products, visiting stores, and making purchases.

Why Nurses like it:

  • Multiple ways to earn
  • Quick rewards
  • Works with many retailers

Tips for Maximizing Cashback

  • Use more than one app.
  • Check offers before shopping.
  • Upload receipts immediately.
  • Watch for bonus promotions.
  • Combine cashback apps with credit card rewards when possible.

Cashback apps won't replace a paycheck, but they can help reduce everyday expenses. For busy nurses looking to stretch their budget, these apps can provide extra savings throughout the year.
