Tips for Maximizing Cashback

  • Use one or two apps at first so the process does not get overwhelming.
  • Check offers before shopping, especially for groceries, gas, and online purchases.
  • Upload receipts as soon as possible so they do not expire.
  • Compare cashback rates before larger online purchases.
  • Only use offers for things you already planned to buy.

Cashback apps will not replace a paycheck, but they can help reduce everyday expenses. Start with the apps that match your normal spending, then add more only if they are worth the extra time.
