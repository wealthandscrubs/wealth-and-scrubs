10 Free Trial Memberships Nurses Should Try

Free trials can be helpful for nurses with busy schedules, but only if you use them on purpose. Before signing up, check the renewal date, monthly price after the trial, cancellation rules, and whether the benefit actually saves time or money for your routine.

1. Amazon Prime

  • 30-day free trial
  • Free shipping
  • Prime Video
  • Prime Reading

2. Walmart+

  • Free trial available
  • Free delivery
  • Fuel discounts
  • Paramount+ included

3. Sam’s Club

  • Frequently offers free or heavily discounted trial memberships
  • Member-only pricing
  • Fuel savings

4. Costco

  • Occasionally offers promotions with gift cards or incentives for new members

5. Audible

  • Free trial with audiobook credits
  • Great for commutes and night-shift recovery days

6. Apple Fitness+

  • Free trial for new users
  • Workout classes
  • Meditation
  • Wellness programs

7. Planet Fitness

  • Often runs free trial passes
  • Useful for nurses wanting affordable fitness options

8. LinkedIn Premium

  • Free trial
  • Learning courses
  • Career tools
  • Resume insights

9. DoorDash Dash Pass

  • Free trial period
  • Reduced delivery fees
  • Useful during long shifts

10. Instacart+

  • Free trial
  • Grocery delivery savings
  • Time-saving convenience for busy nurses

Bonus: Nurse-Specific Free Memberships

Many nurses qualify for free accounts with:

  • ID.me
  • American Nurses Association (member benefits)
  • Sigma Theta Tau International (for eligible nurses)
  • Hospital employee wellness programs

Trial Rule That Saves Headaches

Add the cancellation date to your calendar the same day you sign up. If the trial is not useful by that reminder, cancel before it turns into another forgotten monthly charge.
