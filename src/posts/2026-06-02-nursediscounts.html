Nurse discounts are worth checking before you buy anything you already needed: scrubs, shoes, phone service, travel, education, tech, or everyday basics. The goal is not to collect every offer. The goal is to stop paying full price when a real healthcare-worker perk is available.

Use this guide as a starting point before bigger purchases. Discount rules can change, so always check the current terms and whether the offer applies to nurses, CNAs, LPNs, nursing students, or other healthcare workers.

Scrubs & Shoes

Start here if your work shoes are worn down, your scrubs no longer fit well, or you need a backup set for long stretches of shifts. A small discount on something you wear every week can be more valuable than a flashy one-time deal.

  • Crocs
  • FIGS
  • Adidas
  • Nike

Before buying, compare the healthcare-worker discount against normal sale prices, cashback apps, and shipping costs. If you are still deciding what is actually worth carrying or wearing, start with Affordable Nurse Must-Haves.

Technology

Phone and tech discounts can be especially useful because they apply to bills or products people already pay for. These are worth checking before changing plans, upgrading a phone, buying a laptop, or replacing accessories.

  • Samsung
  • Verizon Nurse Discount
  • AT&T
  • Lenovo

For recurring bills, look at the full monthly cost after taxes, fees, trade-ins, and contract terms. A nurse discount is only helpful if the final plan still fits your budget.

Travel

Travel discounts can help with family trips, conferences, weekend getaways, and travel nursing assignments. Compare healthcare-worker rates with public promo codes, reward member rates, and cashback before booking.

  • Hotels.com
  • Expedia
  • Best Western
  • Choice Hotels

For more travel-specific savings, use Nurse's Guide to Hotel Deals, Rewards & Free Perks and Complete Travel Savings Guide for Nurses.

How to Verify Your Status

Most nurse discounts require verification through platforms such as ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or ExpertVoice. Setting up these accounts once can unlock hundreds of discounts across travel, technology, clothing, and more. 

Tips for Finding More Discounts

  • Always ask if a healthcare worker discount is available.
  • Sign up for store email lists.
  • Check discount websites regularly.
  • Follow your favorite brands on social media.

How to Keep Discounts From Getting Overwhelming

  • Start with the categories you already spend money on, such as shoes, scrubs, phone service, or travel.
  • Keep your badge or verification account ready before major purchases.
  • Compare the discount price against normal sales, cashback portals, and coupon codes.
  • Do not buy something only because it has a nurse discount.

Final Thoughts

Even small discounts can add up over time when you use them on purchases you already planned. Start with one category that affects your real budget, then build the habit of checking nurse discounts before checkout.
