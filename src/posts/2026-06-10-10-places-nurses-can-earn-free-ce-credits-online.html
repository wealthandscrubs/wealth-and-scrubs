Where Nurses Can Find Free Continuing Education (CE) Credits

1. Nurse.com CE Courses

  • Free CE courses available throughout the year
  • State-specific CE options
  • Wide variety of nursing topics

2. CDC TRAIN Learning Network

  • Completely free
  • Infection control
  • Public health
  • Emergency preparedness
  • Earn certificates and CE credits

3. Medscape Education

  • One of the most popular free CE sources
  • Quick courses
  • Pharmacology updates
  • Specialty nursing education

4. AACN Continuing Education

  • Great for critical care nurses
  • Free courses for members
  • Webinars and online education

5. American Nurses Association (ANA) Learning Center

  • Free and discounted CE opportunities
  • Professional development courses
  • Leadership education

6. Elite Learning Free CE Courses

  • Frequently offers free nursing CE credits
  • State-specific requirements
  • Easy online completion

7. Johnson & Johnson Nursing Resources

  • Clinical education
  • Nursing career development
  • Webinars and educational resources

8. Free Nursing CEUs by NurseCE4Less

  • Free CE opportunities
  • Wide variety of topics
  • Instant certificates on many courses

9. Professional Nursing Associations

Many specialty organizations provide free CE credits:

  • Emergency Nurses Association
  • American Association of Critical-Care Nurses
  • Oncology Nursing Society

10. Hospital and Employer Programs

Many hospitals provide:

  • Free CE portals
  • Annual competency education
  • Certification prep courses
  • Webinar libraries
