Hotel costs can take up a big part of a travel budget, especially for family trips, weekend getaways, conferences, or travel nursing assignments. The good news is that there are several ways to compare hotel prices, join free reward programs, and look for deals before you book.

This list can help you compare hotel booking sites, loyalty programs, and cashback options before you reserve a stay.

Hotel Booking Sites to Compare

  • Travelocity: Compare hotels, flights, rental cars, and package deals.
  • CheapTickets: Search hotel deals, flights, vacation packages, and travel discounts.
  • Booking.com: Useful for comparing hotels, apartments, and flexible lodging options.
  • Hotels.com: Helpful for comparing hotel prices and rewards.
  • Expedia: Good for comparing hotels, flights, rental cars, and packages.

Free Hotel Reward Programs

  • Hilton Honors Rewards: Member rates, points, digital check-in, and possible room upgrades.
  • Marriott Bonvoy: Points, free-night redemptions, member rates, and many hotel brands.
  • IHG Rewards Club - Points.com: Rewards for Holiday Inn, Staybridge Suites, Candlewood Suites, and other IHG brands.
  • Wyndham Rewards: Helpful for budget-friendly stays, road trips, and travel assignments.
  • Choice Privileges: Useful for affordable hotels, road trips, and family travel.
  • Best Western Rewards: Good for road trips, smaller towns, and practical hotel stays.
  • World of Hyatt: Valuable if Hyatt locations fit your destination and budget.

Hotel Deal Tips Before Booking

  • Compare the hotel website against at least one booking site.
  • Join the free hotel rewards program before you book.
  • Check whether breakfast, parking, Wi-Fi, or resort fees change the real total.
  • Look for healthcare worker, AAA, military, student, or member rates when available.
  • Compare refundable and nonrefundable rates before choosing the cheapest option.
  • Recheck the price if your trip is still weeks away and cancellation is flexible.

Cashback and Rewards to Check First

  • Rakuten: Check for cashback before booking hotels or buying travel gear.
  • TopCashback: Compare cashback offers for hotel and travel purchases.
  • Fetch: Scan eligible hotel, gas, restaurant, and travel receipts for points.

What to Compare Before You Pay

  • Total nightly rate after taxes and fees
  • Cancellation deadline
  • Parking cost
  • Breakfast or kitchen access
  • Distance from your event, assignment, airport, or attraction
  • Rewards points and cashback options

The best hotel deal is not always the lowest nightly rate. The better choice is usually the stay that gives you the right mix of price, location, flexibility, rewards, and fewer surprise fees.
