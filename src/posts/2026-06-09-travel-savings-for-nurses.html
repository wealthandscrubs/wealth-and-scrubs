Whether you’re planning a family vacation, weekend getaway, cruise, or travel nursing assignment, these resources can help you save money on every trip.

Nurses often have complicated schedules, so travel savings are not only about finding the lowest price. Flexibility, cancellation rules, distance from work or family plans, and whether you can actually rest matter too.

✈️ Flights

Find the Best Flight Deals

Learn how to find cheap flights, use fare alerts, and avoid overpaying for airfare.

  • Compare nearby airports if your schedule allows.
  • Check baggage fees before calling a fare cheap.
  • Use fare alerts for trips you do not need to book immediately.
Compare Travel Deals

🏨 Hotels

Save on Hotels and Resorts

Discover hotel rewards programs, discount booking sites, and ways to earn free nights.

  • Join free hotel rewards programs before booking.
  • Ask about healthcare-worker rates when available.
  • Compare breakfast, parking, and cancellation fees.
Search Hotel Deals

🚗 Rental Cars

Get the Best Rental Car Rates

Compare companies, avoid hidden fees, and learn when to book.

  • Check mileage limits and insurance rules.
  • Compare pickup times against your shift schedule.
  • Recheck rates if your reservation is cancelable.
Compare Rental Cars

🎟️ Attractions & Activities

Save on Things To Do

Theme parks, museums, tours, and local attractions often have discounts available.

Look for healthcare-worker discounts, city passes, local resident deals, employer perks, and off-peak pricing. Do not buy an attraction pass until you know how many activities you will realistically have time to use.
