Looking for ways to stretch your paycheck? These are my favorite money-saving apps for cashback, rewards, coupons, and everyday savings.
As nurses, we work hard for every dollar. Between groceries, gas, bills, uniforms, coffee, and everyday shopping, it adds up fast. That is why I love using money-saving apps that help stretch my paycheck without making life harder.

Money-Saving Apps

  1. Rakuten
  2. Ibotta
  3. Fetch
  4. Upside
  5. ShopKick
  6. Final Thoughts

1. Rakuten

Best For: Online shopping cashback

Rakuten is a free cashback app and browser extension that rewards users for shopping through its platform. When you shop at participating retailers through Rakuten, you earn a percentage of your purchase back as cash.

Rakuten partners with thousands of stores, including popular retailers that nurses frequently use for scrubs, shoes, household items, beauty products, and travel bookings.

Users can receive their earnings through PayPal or a mailed check.

Why Nurses Love It

Nurses often shop online because of busy schedules and long shifts. Whether you’re ordering scrubs, comfortable shoes, gifts, household essentials, or booking travel, Rakuten helps you earn money back on purchases you were already planning to make.

It’s especially useful because it works in the background through a browser extension that automatically alerts you when cashback is available.

Pros

  • Free to use
  • Earn real cash back from thousands of stores
  • Easy browser extension
  • Frequent bonus offers and promotions
  • PayPal or check payouts

Cons

  • Cashback is only available at participating stores
  • Payments are sent quarterly, not instantly
  • You must start your shopping trip through Rakuten to earn rewards

Nurse Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

My Take: Rakuten is one of the easiest ways for nurses to save money online. If you regularly buy scrubs, shoes, household items, or travel accommodations online, Rakuten can help you earn cash back with almost no extra effort. It’s one of the first money-saving apps I recommend because it’s simple, free, and can add up over time.

2. Ibotta

Best For: Grocery cashback

Ibotta is a free cashback app that rewards users for purchasing groceries, household items, beauty products, and other everyday essentials. Simply browse available offers, shop as usual, upload your receipt, and earn cash back.

You can cash out your earnings through PayPal, bank transfer, or gift cards once you reach the minimum payout amount.

Why Nurses Love It

Between long shifts and busy schedules, nurses often spend a significant portion of their budget on groceries and household necessities. Ibotta helps stretch every dollar by offering cash back on items you’re already buying.

Whether you’re meal prepping for the week, stocking up on snacks for night shifts, or shopping for your family, Ibotta can help lower your grocery bill.

Pros

  • Free to use
  • Real cash back on groceries and household items
  • Works with many major retailers
  • Cash out to PayPal or bank account
  • Frequent bonus offers

Cons

  • Must activate offers before shopping
  • Receipts must be uploaded within the required timeframe
  • Not every product qualifies for cashback

Nurse Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

My Take: Ibotta is one of the best apps for nurses looking to reduce grocery expenses. Combined with Fetch Rewards and store sales, it can help you save hundreds of dollars each year on purchases you were already planning to make.

3. Fetch

Best For: Earning rewards from everyday receipts

Fetch Rewards is a free app that lets you earn points by simply scanning receipts from grocery stores, restaurants, gas stations, pharmacies, and many other retailers.

After earning enough points, you can redeem them for gift cards to popular stores like Amazon, Target, Walmart, Starbucks, and more.

Why Nurses Love It

After a long shift, the last thing most nurses want to do is clip coupons or search for deals. Fetch makes saving money easy by rewarding purchases you’re already making.

Whether you’re buying groceries for your family, grabbing coffee before work, or picking up household essentials, you can earn points just by scanning your receipt.

Pros

  • Free to use
  • Any receipt can earn points
  • Takes less than a minute to scan
  • Large selection of gift cards
  • Bonus points on participating brands

Cons

  • Rewards can take time to build up
  • Points expire if the account is inactive for long periods
  • Gift cards only (no direct cash payout)

Nurse Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

My Take: Fetch is one of the easiest money-saving apps available. It requires very little effort and rewards nurses for purchases they’re already making every day. It’s a great “set it and forget it” app for busy healthcare workers.

4. Upside

Best For: Saving money on gas

Upside is a cashback app that helps users earn money back on gas purchases. In some areas, it also offers cashback on groceries and restaurants.
Simply:

  1. Open the app.
  2. Claim an offer at a nearby gas station.
  3. Pay as usual.
  4. Upload a receipt or pay with a linked card.
  5. Receive cashback.

Why Nurses Love It

Many nurses commute long distances, work at multiple facilities, or pick up extra shifts. Gas expenses can add up quickly. Upside helps nurses earn cash back on fuel they were already going to buy.

Whether you’re driving to a hospital, nursing home, clinic, or home health visit, Upside can help reduce transportation costs.

Pros

  • Free to use
  • Real cash back on gas purchases
  • Some grocery and restaurant offers available
  • Cash out through PayPal, bank transfer, or gift cards

Cons

  • Cashback amounts vary by location
  • Not all gas stations participate
  • Offers must be claimed before purchasing fuel

Nurse Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

My Take: If I could only recommend one money-saving app to nurses, Upside would be near the top of the list because most nurses drive regularly and can start saving money immediately with very little effort.

5. ShopKick

Best For: Earning rewards while shopping

  • Walking into stores
  • Scanning products
  • Uploading receipts
  • Shopping online
  • Linking eligible purchases

You can redeem kicks for gift cards to stores like Amazon, Walmart, Target, and Starbucks.

Why nurses like it:

Many nurses already stop at places like Walmart, Target, CVS, Walgreens, and grocery stores on the way home from work. Shopkick lets you earn rewards during those normal shopping trips without spending extra money.

For nurses with long shifts and busy schedules, it’s an easy way to collect points while running errands they were already going to do.

Pros

  • Free to use
  • Multiple ways to earn rewards
  • Gift card payouts
  • Works with stores nurses frequently visit

Cons

  • Rewards accumulate slowly
  • Some offers require scanning products in-store
  • Not every store participates

Nurse Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ (4/5)

Final Thoughts

You do not need to use every app at once. Start with two or three that fit your life best. For nurses, I would start with Upside for gas, Fetch Rewards for receipts, and Rakuten for online shopping.

Small savings may not feel like much at first, but over time they can help you keep more of your hard-earned money.
