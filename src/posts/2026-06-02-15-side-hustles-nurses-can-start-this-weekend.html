Looking for extra income as a nurse? Whether you're an LPN, RN, CNA, or nursing student, there are many ways to earn money outside of your regular shifts. Here are 15 side hustles nurses can start this weekend. Some can generate money immediately, while others can grow into long-term income streams. For more money topics, start with the Money & Side Hustles hub.

1. Per-Diem Nursing

Pick up extra shifts when you want them and earn a higher hourly rate.

2. Flu Shot Clinics

Many pharmacies and healthcare organizations hire nurses for seasonal vaccination clinics.

3. Health Screenings

Community health fairs often need nurses to perform blood pressure and wellness checks.

4. Medical Surveys

Some companies pay healthcare professionals for their opinions on products and services.

5. Affiliate Marketing

Recommend products online and earn a commission when someone purchases through your link.

6. Blogging

A website can become a long-term source of income through ads and affiliate programs.

7. Freelance Health Writing

Many healthcare websites pay nurses to write educational content.

8. CPR Instruction

Teach CPR classes after becoming a certified instructor.

9. Medication Management

Help families organize medications and health records.

10. Childcare for Healthcare Workers

Offer flexible childcare that fits around nursing schedules.

11. Pet Sitting

A great option if you enjoy animals and want a flexible side income.

12. Medical Transcription

Review and transcribe medical notes from home.

13. Selling Digital Products

Create planners, checklists, or nursing resources and sell them online.

14. Tutoring Nursing Students

Help students prepare for exams and clinicals.

15. Cashback and Rewards Apps

Earn money back on purchases you already make.

Final Thoughts

The best side hustle is the one that fits your lifestyle and schedule. Start with one opportunity, learn it well, and build from there.
