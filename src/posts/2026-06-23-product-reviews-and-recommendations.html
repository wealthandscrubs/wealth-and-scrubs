Not every product, app, or service is worth your money. Product Reviews & Recommendations is where I’ll share practical finds that may help make work, home, self-care, and everyday spending a little easier.

As Wealth & Scrubs grows, this section will focus on items and resources that are useful, realistic, and budget-conscious. The goal is not to recommend more things just for the sake of buying. The goal is to help you choose better when something can truly save time, reduce stress, stretch your money, or improve your routine.

How I Choose Recommendations

Before something earns a place here, I look for products and resources that are useful, easy to understand, and worth the time or money. I also consider whether the item fits real life, especially for healthcare workers, busy parents, students, and anyone trying to make practical choices on a budget.

Some recommendations may come from personal use. Others may come from research, comparison, reader needs, or trusted resources I’ve found helpful over the years.
