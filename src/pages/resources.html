Start with the biggest savings

  • Compare flights and lodging before locking in your dates.
  • Join free hotel and airline rewards programs before you book.
  • Ask about healthcare worker rates and flexible cancellation policies.
  • Check the total price, including baggage, parking, taxes, and resort fees.

Smart ways to save on every trip

✈️ Flights

Use fare alerts, compare nearby airports, and check whether a carry-on or checked bag changes the real cost.

🏨 Hotels

Compare member rates, healthcare discounts, free breakfast, parking, and cancellation terms before booking.

🚗 Rental Cars

Price the full rental, review fuel and toll policies, and recheck the rate as your travel date approaches.

🎟️ Attractions

Look for advance-purchase prices, city passes, healthcare discounts, and free museum days.

💳 Travel Rewards

Use points carefully, compare cash value, and avoid spending more simply to earn a reward.

📱 Helpful Apps

Keep itineraries organized and use trusted tools for fare tracking, maps, gas prices, and cashback.

Before you book

  • Confirm the cancellation and change policy.
  • Compare the final price, not just the advertised rate.
  • Ask whether a nurse or healthcare worker discount is available.
  • Check your employer, professional association, and existing memberships for travel benefits.
  • Keep confirmation numbers and receipts together for easy access.

Travel offers and eligibility can change. Always review the provider's current terms before booking.
