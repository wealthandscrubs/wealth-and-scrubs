Our Mission

To help healthcare professionals and everyday families save more, earn more, and build a life they love.

Hi, I'm Brooke.

I'm an LPN, mom, and healthcare professional who understands how difficult it can be to balance work, family, and finances. I created Wealth & Scrubs to share practical ways to save money, build financial confidence, discover side hustles, and make life a little easier for healthcare workers and beyond.

My goal is to help others create more freedom, less stress, and a better future—one smart money move at a time.

Wealth & Scrubs was created from my passion for helping others improve their finances, find opportunities to earn extra income, and build a life with more freedom and less financial stress. Whether you're in healthcare or simply looking for practical money-saving tips, you're welcome here.
