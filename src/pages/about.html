Building Wealth Beyond the Bedside

Welcome to Wealth & Scrubs. I'm Brooke, an LPN, mom, and healthcare professional who knows how challenging it can be to balance long shifts, family life, bills, goals, and the everyday pressure to make your money stretch further.

I created Wealth & Scrubs to help healthcare workers, busy parents, and everyday readers save more, earn more, and feel more confident with money. This site is here to make financial wellness feel practical, approachable, and realistic for real life.

Here, you'll find helpful resources for:

  • Saving money on everyday expenses
  • Finding deals, discounts, rewards, and freebies
  • Exploring side hustles and extra income ideas
  • Learning beginner-friendly financial tips
  • Making home, travel, self-care, and busy routines more affordable
  • Navigating healthcare life with more support and less stress

My mission is to help you create more breathing room in your budget, more confidence in your choices, and more freedom for the life you are building. Whether you work in healthcare or simply want practical ways to save and live better, you're welcome here.

Stay Connected

Whether you're a healthcare professional, a busy parent, or simply looking for ways to save more and live better, Wealth & Scrubs is here to help.
