Why We Use Affiliate Links

At Wealth & Scrubs, transparency is important to us.

We only recommend products, services, and resources that we believe may be helpful to our readers. Any earnings received through affiliate partnerships help support the operation of this website and allow us to continue creating free content.

Your trust is important to us, and we are committed to providing honest opinions, reviews, and recommendations.

We appreciate your support and are committed to keeping Wealth & Scrubs a trusted resource for healthcare professionals and everyday families.
