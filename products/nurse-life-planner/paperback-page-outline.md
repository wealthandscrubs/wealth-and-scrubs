# Paperback Planner Page Outline

## Page 1: Weekly Shift Planner

Purpose:

Help nurses see the week at a glance and plan work, home, meals, and recovery time.

Fields:

- Week of
- Main focus this week
- Monday through Sunday schedule blocks
- Shift time
- Unit / facility
- Priority tasks
- Appointments
- Meals / snacks to pack
- Workout / movement
- Sleep goal
- Water goal
- Notes

Footer prompt:

What would make this week feel easier?

## Page 2: Shift Log

Purpose:

Track each worked shift for memory, pay, patterns, and reflection.

Fields:

- Date
- Facility / unit
- Shift type: day, evening, night, PRN, overtime, holiday
- Start time
- End time
- Break length
- Total hours
- Mood / energy before shift
- Mood / energy after shift
- Important notes
- Anything to follow up on
- One thing that went well

Footer prompt:

What do I need before my next shift?

## Page 3: Paycheck Estimate Tracker

Purpose:

Help estimate gross pay before payday.

Fields:

- Pay period start
- Pay period end
- Pay date
- Base hourly rate
- Regular hours
- Overtime hours
- Shift differential hours
- Holiday hours
- Bonus / incentive pay
- Estimated gross pay
- Deductions estimate
- Estimated take-home pay
- Actual take-home pay
- Difference

Footer prompt:

Where should this paycheck go first?

## Page 4: Overtime and Differential Tracker

Purpose:

Track extra earnings from overtime, night shift, weekend differential, holiday pay, and bonuses.

Fields:

- Date
- Shift type
- Extra rate or differential
- Extra hours
- Estimated extra pay
- Reason for extra shift
- Was it worth it?

Monthly summary:

- Total overtime hours
- Total differential pay
- Total bonus pay
- Total extra income
- Money goal funded by extra income

Footer prompt:

Am I using extra shifts to support my goals or cover burnout?

## Page 5: Post-Shift Reset Checklist

Purpose:

Give nurses a simple decompression routine after hard shifts.

Checklist:

- Drink water
- Eat something nourishing
- Change out of work clothes
- Shower or wash face
- Set out essentials for next shift
- Write down anything that needs follow-up
- Put phone on quiet
- Stretch for 5 minutes
- Do one calming thing
- Go to sleep without guilt

Reflection:

- Today felt
- My body needs
- My mind needs
- One thing I am proud of
- One thing I can release

Footer prompt:

Caring for others starts with caring for yourself.
