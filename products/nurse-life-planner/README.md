# Wealth & Scrubs Nurse Life Planner

## Product Idea

The Wealth & Scrubs Nurse Life Planner is a nurse-focused planner and shift tracker designed for real healthcare schedules. It should help nurses track shifts, money, overtime, reminders, meals, goals, and recovery time without requiring another monthly subscription.

Core positioning:

> Plan your shifts, track your pay, and organize your life without another monthly app fee.

## Product Formats

### 1. Paperback Planner

Best first product because it can be designed, tested, and sold faster.

Current product files in this folder include the source sample pages, CNA and LPN planner variants, printable PDFs, and `wealth-and-scrubs-nurse-life-planner-sample-pages.pdf`.

Possible formats:

- Amazon KDP paperback
- Printable PDF
- Digital PDF for tablets
- Bundle with habit trackers or nurse-specific inserts

Suggested size:

- 8.5 x 11 inches for roomy writing space
- Black-and-white interior first to keep printing costs lower
- Color cover with Wealth & Scrubs branding

### 2. One-Time Payment App

The app should avoid subscriptions. A simple paid download or one-time unlock fits the brand promise.

Possible pricing:

- $4.99 simple starter version
- $9.99 full version
- Optional future upgrades as separate one-time add-ons, not monthly billing

## Target User

Primary:

- Nurses
- LPNs
- RNs
- CNAs
- Nursing students
- Travel nurses
- PRN/per diem nurses

Secondary:

- Healthcare workers with rotating schedules
- Busy moms in healthcare
- Shift workers who need pay and schedule organization

## What Makes It Different

- Built for 12-hour shifts, night shift, PRN work, overtime, and real nurse life.
- Combines schedule, money, wellness, and home organization in one place.
- No monthly app subscription.
- Paperback option for people who like writing things down.
- Can connect naturally to Wealth & Scrubs posts, affiliate links, Pinterest, and digital products.
