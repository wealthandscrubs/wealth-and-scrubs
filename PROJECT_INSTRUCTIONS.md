# Wealth & Scrubs

## Website Management Goals
- Increase qualified organic and social traffic.
- Improve useful, editorially relevant affiliate clicks.
- Strengthen technical SEO, internal linking, and search-focused content quality.
- Maintain a clean, fast, accessible, and trustworthy user experience.
- Prioritize reader value over link volume or promotional pressure.

## Audience
Nurses and everyday people looking to save money.

## Writing Style
- Friendly
- SEO optimized
- Easy to read
- Use headings and bullet points

## Content Rules
- Create posts that help people save money, earn money, travel for less, and find discounts.
- Target both nurses and the general public.
- Write at least 1,000-1,500 words per post.
- Use headings, bullet points, and tables when helpful.
- Add an FAQ section to every post.
- Avoid duplicate content.

## Categories
- Deals & Discounts
- Travel Savings
- Money & Side Hustles
- Nursing Life
- Lifestyle & Self Care
- Home & Organization

## Internal Linking Rules
- Add 3-8 internal links to related articles whenever possible.
- Link category pages to relevant posts.
- Build topic clusters around all six site categories.

## Affiliate Rules
- Treat the current Impact `My Brands` list as the sole allowlist for Impact advertisers.
- Treat affiliate links manually supplied by the site owner as verified links for the named brands, including links from other networks such as CJ or direct referral programs.
- Preserve manually supplied affiliate links unless the site owner replaces or removes them.
- Only use affiliate links for advertisers whose approval and tracking URL have been verified.
- Insert affiliate links only when they are directly relevant to the surrounding content.
- Do not create articles, pages, or social posts around brands that are not approved.
- Do not treat a blank link, a "link needed" note, or a brand idea as an approval.
- Keep affiliate links editorially natural; never force a brand mention solely to add a link.
- Maintain a master affiliate database.
- Update links when advertisers change.
- Include affiliate disclosures when appropriate.

## SEO Rules
- Create SEO-friendly titles and meta descriptions.
- Use concise, descriptive URLs.
- Add meaningful image alt text.
- Create a table of contents for long posts.

## Website Rules
- Never delete an existing post without approval.
- Back up files before major changes.
- Check for broken links after relevant changes.
- Keep navigation simple and mobile-friendly.

## Social Media Rules
- Generate a Pinterest description for each article.
- Generate an X post for each article.
- Suggest one featured image for each article.

## Growth Rules
- Identify content gaps and suggest articles related to existing content.
- Prioritize useful articles with strong search demand and relevant affiliate potential.
- Do not compromise accuracy, reader trust, or relevance for commission potential.
